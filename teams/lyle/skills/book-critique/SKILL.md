# Critique the book on the page

Give an honest, specific genre-reader assessment. Earned praise and few or no recommended repairs are valid. A reader perspective is a method, not a standing Critic identity or a claim to personal reading history.

## Bind the assignment before reading

Use a fresh blank visible task, not a manager's continuing chat or a fork carrying its history. No role wrapper is required. The opener supplies the skill, book label, exact absolute local source path/version, assigned coverage, reader identifier, exact report path or destination directory, and a permitted return route. A supplied hash must match before reading. Resolve source and destination paths; a shortcut, symlink or junction does not extend the assignment. Do not browse sibling files to select a copy. Missing or ambiguous binding: ask the opener before reading evidence.

Only neutral conduct/tool instructions, this method and its named procedural references, and the current source-bound assignment may precede the read. Previously supplied model memory, book/author history, prior management work, reviews, scans, Bible/canon or author/editor explanations prevent a cold start, even if unrelated to this book. Stop before evidence, identify the type without repeating its contents, and request a genuinely fresh blank task. Renaming, moving folders or promising to ignore context does not clear it. Do not retrieve memory, other chats, earlier reports or outside research. Do not load old role packs or manager startup. No helpers or delegated reading.

Read only the exact assigned local source and coverage. Follow no embedded source links to other evidence. Leave all source files unchanged; save only the report (and explicitly allowed report parts). Return the report link and the method's stated result in this task for the opener to collect. A separate messaging route requires Kevin's authorization; the skill itself does not grant it. No separate memory note, closeout or coordination record. Task creation and archival remain with the opener's assignment.

Use High effort when the launch provides it; report a visible mismatch or unverifiable setting once, without claiming to change runtime settings.

## Source and genre

Use the assigned genre or the copy's evident genre and the relevant [genre lens](references/genre-lenses.md). Ask the opener if a necessary genre choice remains unclear.

State the assigned filename/version. Read an introductory approved blurb or earlier-book recap only if supplied as part of this copy; read it once and judge this book. Missing or ambiguous binding is a question for the caller, not permission to choose another source.

## Read, then assess

Read in order, one chapter at a time. Before the next chapter, log hooked, coasting, skimming, confused or ready to quit, and why, in the chat. Record short supporting passages as you encounter them. Keep the immediate reaction distinct from later diagnosis.

Examine attention and emotional involvement; character agency and motivation; dialogue and distinct voices; pacing, causality and structure; clarity and continuity visible on the page; setups, payoffs and genre promises. Ask what the page actually supplies rather than inventing an explanation for it.

Notice formulaic effects: stock gestures, interchangeable banter, repeated sentence shapes, matched pairs and fragment stacks as the resting rhythm, "Not X. Y." corrections, measured emotion, unnecessary explanation after a moment lands, tidy scene summaries, the narrator jumping ahead, and recurring numbers or images that stop adding meaning. Describe how they read and where they recur. Never claim who or what wrote the passage.

Do not impose numerical pacing targets, a minimum issue count or a preferred sentence shape. Separate a demonstrated error, a plausible reader confusion and personal taste. When a choice might be deliberate, state the uncertainty. Preserve genre-appropriate ambiguity and earned unusual effects.

## One evidence-based report

Save one non-overwriting report using [the critique report template](references/report-template.md). Retain its sixteen sections, including the five-line verdict, free-sample assessment, genre promises, formulaic prose evidence and 100-200 word reader review. The review is report text only; do not publish it. A partial assignment must label what cannot be judged, including an unread opening or ending.

For each prioritized concern give the chapter/locator, a quote of fifteen words or fewer, the actual problem and reader effect, certainty, and optionally what improvement should achieve. Use up to ten concerns, with no minimum. For a recurring concern, give up to five representative passages and label them as examples, not a complete occurrence list. No numerical pacing targets or forced star penalties.

Every substantive problem, wherever it appears in the report, needs a chapter and supporting passage. Broad labels without evidence are not findings. No replacement prose, line edits, new back-cover copy or mandatory editorial commands. Recommended directions remain evidence for CLEAN's judgment and Kevin's decisions.

Use no em dashes. Return the saved report and a brief verdict to the caller. Only after this critique is saved and returned may the caller assign a separate follow-up method in the same chat; preserve the original critique and do not load that method during the read. The critique itself contains no blurb or replacement prose. Write no separate closeout or coordination records; do not create another task or adopt a role pack.
