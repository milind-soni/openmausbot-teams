## Stop before errors multiply
Before a full art run, review one representative difficult spread at normal reading size plus useful close-ups. Test the risk that could repeat: likeness transfer, adult lap anatomy, hide visibility, character identity or layout. An existing accepted pilot can satisfy this gate after its source and approval are verified.
Then release only a named bounded batch: roughly one fifth or sixth of the book, with spreads kept whole; use a smaller batch when the risk warrants it. Stop after every batch for coordinator inspection and explicit release. Each new art part is a separately bounded subtask in the book's existing workflow; do not create duplicate book organizers. Assignment must name exact pages/masters, maximum work and protected content. No implied next-batch release.
A pilot failure pauses propagation. Fix that pilot and inspect it again. Two failed attempts at the same defect require coordinator reassessment of the method before further generation; do not repeat an unchanged failing prompt.
For a local repair permitted by the assignment and owning art method, start from a bounded crop or mask and composite the reviewed repair into the original. Storybook/Etsy face repairs follow [art-making](C:/Users/kehar/Documents/SOPS/SKILLS/art-making/SKILL.md#fixing-a-page) and art-check: never paste a regenerated face onto an old page; redraw the whole page or figure with the locked portrait. Apply this generic crop/composite route only where those specific rules permit it. Verify unchanged pixels outside the allowed region. A full-scene generation can change protected content despite a preservation prompt; do not repeat that method after it fails. Inside the patch still needs visual review for anatomy, likeness, occlusion, seams and lighting.
During a sequential pickup run, source preflight may proceed in other book chats, but artwork starts only when the coordinator releases that book.

## Inspect the actual product
Open the real images and rendered pages, not only prompts, descriptions or contact sheets. Contact sheets test sequence and consistency; full pages and close-ups test details.
Apply only relevant checks:
- Likeness/style: compare references directly. Check apparent age, eye/nose/mouth relationships, hair, skin tone, expression and head scale. Natural proportions plus visible pencil marks; avoid cartoon enlargement and photographic pasted faces. Pose and lighting legitimately change appearance. Visual estimates are not precise measurements.
- Anatomy/integration: complete plausible heads, necks, shoulders, laps, limbs and hands; believable contact, perspective, head/body fit, edges and light. Deliberate crops must read naturally. Avoid headless sweater bodies.
- Story/continuity: required action, right character and prop, clothing and colors, scene order, relation to exact approved words. Check each gender/version where required. No silent rewrite of story text.
- Grace, when required: compare supplied likeness; silvery-gray fur, brown collar with pink hearts, one intended hide per assigned scene, genuinely concealed yet findable at reading size. Keep approved hiding locations unless the assignment changes them. White/cream substitutions or an obvious foreground dog fail the November 5 pickup brief. Do not add Grace to unrelated work.
- Layout/covers: readable hierarchy, contrast, spacing, unclipped text, safe areas, spreads/gutter, consistent fonts, correct title/names/page order, intended trim. Inspect front/back and all revised pages.
- Content: compare extracted final text with the approved wording; distinguish authorized name changes from drift. Verify puzzles/keys/links or personalization with a representative alternate name when applicable.
- Files: reopen final exports; verify dimensions, page count/order, actual file size and format-specific requirements. Each Etsy digital file must be below 20,000,000 bytes for this workflow. Check compressed delivered files visually. Print readiness requires the actual binding/template specifications and evidence, not a generic PDF pass.
- Preservation: for bounded edits compare before/after and verify unaffected pages/regions using pixel/content checks where practical. Inspect changes caused by re-rendering instead of assuming they are harmless.
For PDFs or other specialist formats use the relevant available artifact skill and tools. Missing tools or unreadable artifacts mean a reported verification limit, never an invented pass.

## Verdict and handoff
Return Pass, Fix, or Needs approval for the exact checkpoint. Give each material finding a page/image locator, visible evidence and smallest required correction. Mark uncertainty explicitly; no invented accuracy percentages or arbitrary aesthetic scores. A beautiful picture does not compensate for the wrong character, broken anatomy, unreadable text or failed likeness.
Record once in the existing production record: exact reviewed files/version, sources, pages inspected, checks, findings, held work and next release. Preserve prior versions for the assigned work. Customer-order contact sheets, rendered pages, review artifacts and personalized finals stay in that order's temporary folder and follow [the order SOP's delivery and cleanup rule](C:/Users/kehar/Documents/SOPS/STORYBOOKS/SOP-STORYBOOK-ORDER.md); do not copy them to the reusable product shelf, template library or a separate review/day-folder archive. Retain only the permitted non-identifying order record after cleanup. For reusable book-template handoffs, provide the contact sheet and review artifact in that book's own PROJECTS/ETSY/STORYBOOKS folder; other reusable products use their PROJECTS/ETSY/OTHER folder, and app assets use the assigned app folder. Keep the chat summary short.
A Pass applies only to the checked batch and format. It does not imply full-book approval, print proof, Etsy publication, a purchase or release of a different held task. Final approval remains with Kevin unless he explicitly delegated that approval.
