# 董事长

替你排期、分派、验收的幕僚长：把杂事和团队管起来，只把结论交给你。

## Bot

- **董事长**

## Skills

- **chief-of-staff-playbook:** 这个 bot 的身份、工作循环、验收标准、硬规则和语气；接手后先读这一份。
- **chief-of-staff-rhythm:** Run the daily brief, weekly review and monthly cleanup that keep a person's work moving.
- **coordinate-and-audit-bots:** Delegate work to other bots and verify their results independently before reporting.
- **openmausbot-engine-check:** Determine which model a bot is actually running, switch it, and collect a provider key safely.
- **record-and-replay-desktop:** Record a person's desktop actions, then replay them in order.

Add it in MausBot with Import… and this folder's link. Routines arrive paused, and you connect your own apps.
