# Read for the experience

Read for pleasure through the assigned genre lens and report what happened to your attention, understanding and feelings. The lens is a reading method, not a persistent identity or an invented personal history.

## Bind the assignment before reading

Use a fresh blank visible task, not a manager's continuing chat or a fork carrying its history. No role wrapper is required. The opener supplies the skill, book label, exact absolute local source path/version, assigned coverage, reader identifier, exact report path or destination directory, and a permitted return route. A supplied hash must match before reading. Resolve source and destination paths; a shortcut, symlink or junction does not extend the assignment. Do not browse sibling files to select a copy. Missing or ambiguous binding: ask the opener before reading evidence.

Only neutral conduct/tool instructions, this method and its named procedural references, and the current source-bound assignment may precede the read. Previously supplied model memory, book/author history, prior management work, reviews, scans, Bible/canon or author/editor explanations prevent a cold start, even if unrelated to this book. Stop before evidence, identify the type without repeating its contents, and request a genuinely fresh blank task. Renaming, moving folders or promising to ignore context does not clear it. Do not retrieve memory, other chats, earlier reports or outside research. Do not load old role packs or manager startup. No helpers or delegated reading.

Read only the exact assigned local source and coverage. Follow no embedded source links to other evidence. Leave all source files unchanged; save only the report (and explicitly allowed report parts). Return the report link and the method's stated result in this task for the opener to collect. A separate messaging route requires Kevin's authorization; the skill itself does not grant it. No separate memory note, closeout or coordination record. Task creation and archival remain with the opener's assignment.

Use High effort when the launch provides it; report a visible mismatch or unverifiable setting once, without claiming to change runtime settings.

## Source and genre

State the exact assigned file/version once. If the copy includes an approved introductory blurb or earlier-book recap, read it once first as part of this copy. Judge the assigned book, not those earlier books. Do not return to the recap. Use the assigned genre, or the evident genre of the copy; an ambiguous source or necessary genre choice goes back to the caller. Read as a fan of that genre without inventing an age, biography or personal reading history.

## Read in order

Read one chapter at a time, or only the exact assigned range. Before opening the next chapter, write one immediate log line in the chat:

`Chapter | how I feel | hooked/coasting/skimming/confused/ready to quit | what I expect next | question I most want answered`

Include the short phrase or moment that caused a notable reaction while it is in front of you, no more than fifteen quoted words. At roughly 10%, halfway and 85%, also record your guess about the ending. For a partial assignment, label checkpoints relative to that range and do not pretend to have read the book's ending.

Do not reread earlier chapters to repair an experience of confusion or forgotten detail. That loss is part of the result. Keep the chapter logs in the chat until the final report; do not make additional running-log files. Do not provide fixes, grades, stars, line edits or a technical explanation of why the prose failed.

## Report from memory

After reading, write the report from memory and your recorded reactions without reopening the manuscript. Do not invent exact wording you failed to retain. A missing exact quote is a stated evidence limit, not permission to fabricate one.

Save one report using [the beta report template](references/report-template.md), at the exact assigned path or with its non-overwriting filename convention in the assigned destination. Retain all twelve sections and label unavailable evidence honestly.

Tie substantive reactions to chapters and retained short quotations or clearly identified moments. Describe the effect on you in plain reader language. Do not turn a plausibility question into a demand that the manuscript explain the research. Honest enthusiasm, boredom, confusion and few or no complaints are all valid.

Use no em dashes in the report. Return the report path and the Would you keep going section to the caller. This reading/report assignment creates no rewritten prose, blurb, separate closeout or other records. Only after that report is saved and returned may the caller assign a separate follow-up method in this same chat; keep the original report fixed and do not load the follow-up before finishing it. Task creation, return routing and archival belong to the caller's assignment, not this skill.
