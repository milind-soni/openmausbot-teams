# Make Pinterest pins

This method plans and prepares one assigned Pinterest batch (or a board plan, keyword study or results review) for the shop's Etsy listings and returns checked files. The goal is free Pinterest traffic to the shop. It confers no account, posting or spending authority; those stay with Kevin, as set out under Limits.

## Limits

These hold for every use of this skill.

- **Posting needs Kevin's yes.** Publishing or scheduling pins, making or changing boards on the live account, and any account change each need Kevin's own yes for that action. A yes covers the batch and boards he named, not later ones. Drafting pins, board plans and upload files needs no separate yes.
- **No spending.** No Pinterest ads or promoted pins without a budget and a yes from Kevin.
- **Account steps are Kevin's.** The business account, linking or claiming the shop, and signing in. Never create an account or sign in. When Kevin needs steps, give short ones you have checked on the live site; label any guess as a guess.
- **Pinterest only.** No Facebook, Instagram or Kevin's personal social media. Other platforms, listing changes, product art and our novels belong elsewhere; say so in one line and leave it.
- **Listings are read-only.** Use the live listings, their photos and links. Never change a listing. Ask the Etsy side for anything you need.
- **No art.** Use only approved listing photos and existing videos (such as listing flip videos). Make no new pictures, AI or otherwise. Pin layout (for example a title band on one of our photos) is this method's call.
- **Public samples need public-use approval.** No customer photo or private family reference is ever pinned. A picture or book that shows a real child, Kevin's son included, is a public sample only when Kevin has approved that picture or book for public use.
- **Trademark.** Never use the "Elf on the Shelf" name.

- The current account record: boards, house pin style and what is already posted, in the record the assignment names (currently the [Pinterest notes](C:/Users/kehar/Documents/PROJECTS/_SYSTEM/workers/PROJECTS-PINTEREST/NOTES.md) and the batch READMEs under [ETSY/SHOP/Pinterest](C:/Users/kehar/Documents/PROJECTS/ETSY/SHOP/Pinterest/)). Don't pin a listing twice by accident.

## Plan

1. **Season first.** Christmas items while Christmas is on. Use Pinterest Trends for rising searches and their forecast peaks, and pin ahead of the peak.
2. **Keywords.** Build each title and description around what shoppers actually search, from Trends and Pinterest search suggestions. Plain words, no keyword stuffing.
3. **Boards.** Fit each pin to an existing board where one fits. Propose a new board (name and description) only when a group of pins needs one; making it live needs Kevin's yes.
4. **A few strong pins at a time,** spread across listings, not a flood. Hold any listing that is not yet live and buyable, and give no story spoilers.

Use Pinterest's own current help pages for specs, rules and how-tos, including its rules on AI-made images. Record each page's link and the date you checked it. Specs change, so check them each batch rather than trusting an old number.

## Prepare the batch

Save into one dated, versioned batch folder (as the earlier batches do). Never overwrite an approved or posted version.

- Tall pin images from approved listing photos, at Pinterest's current recommended ratio. Video pins from existing videos, framed to fit Pinterest's current video ratio limit.
- `PIN-TEXT.md`: for each pin, the title, description, keywords, board and the exact Etsy listing link.
- A preview sheet showing every pin with its text, for review.
- `README.md`: what is in the batch, which listings, what was held and why, the help pages checked, and what needs Kevin's yes.

## Check before returning

1. Every link opens the right live listing, and every listing still adds to cart.
2. Every image and video is an approved shop asset approved for public use, with no customer or private family photos and no new art.
3. Text matches the listing and the shop's wording rules; no trademarked names; no spoilers.
4. Each pin's board exists or is listed as a proposal.

## Posting, only after Kevin's yes

- Bulk CSV upload needs public media links, so files on this PC go up through Create Pin, about 10 at a time.
- In Create Pin, plain inputs (title, link) can take text by script, but the Description box needs a real click and typing; script-inserted text left it blank and the draft stuck on "Saving...". Check every field by script before Publish, then check that the form cleared.
- Follow Pinterest's current disclosure rules for AI-made images, checked on its help pages for this batch. Where a label is required, apply it. Where it is optional, the choice is Kevin's; ask if the assignment doesn't say.
- A scheduled pin can go out early with ... > Publish now. Pinterest allows few changes to a scheduled pin, so check before scheduling.
- Record each posted pin (date, pin, board, link) in the assignment's named record.

## Results

When Kevin shares numbers, say plainly which pins and boards bring clicks to Etsy, what to keep and what to drop. Recommend ads only after free pins show clicks, and only as a recommendation for Kevin.

## Return

Return the batch folder path, the checks done, what was held and why, and the exact actions that need Kevin's yes. For posted work, also the posted record. Self-check and fix first; return finished, checked work or a real blocker through the assignment.
