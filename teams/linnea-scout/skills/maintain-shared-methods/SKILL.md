# Maintain shared methods

Read [Creating and maintaining our methods](../../SOP-SKILL-CREATION.md) for the authoring and verification process. SOPs use the [common seven-section template](../../templates/SOP-TEMPLATE.md); keep its core headings and order, with process-specific subsections as needed. Use the [methods directory](../../INDEX.md) to find the existing owner before writing another procedure.

Apply that SOP to the exact assigned files and outcome. In Codex, use the installed skill-creator for package-format work; in another app use its supported authoring guidance. Keep shared execution instructions at their canonical home and make role, SOP and app-discovery callers point there.

Return the changed canonical methods, repaired callers, relevant verification and any actual unresolved decision through the current assignment. The library's draft, retired and project-specific methods retain their status and source boundaries.
